What we check on every single job

  • 01
    Is the employer licensed, right now? Matched against the current Home Office register of licensed sponsors — A-rated, on the Skilled Worker route.
  • 02
    Have they sponsored consistently? We hold four years of register snapshots. An employer who appeared once last year is a different risk from one present every year.
  • 03
    Can this salary legally be sponsored? Checked against the going rate for that occupation code, not the headline general threshold. This is the check nothing else does.
  • 04
    Does the ad rule you out anyway? Security clearance, existing right to work, nationality requirements, contract and day-rate roles that a visa can't cover.
  • 05
    Is the job actually in the UK? "York" is not New York. "Cambridge, MA" is not Cambridge. Job boards get this wrong constantly and it wastes your week.
